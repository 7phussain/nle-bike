What is the range of ENGWE e-bikes? ▼

ENGWE e-bikes offer different ranges depending on the model:

  • ENGWE Engine X: Up to 75 miles (120km) in eco mode
  • ENGWE Engine Pro 2.0: Up to 90 miles (145km) in eco mode

  • ENGWE M20: Up to 70 miles (112km) in eco mode

Range varies based on rider weight, terrain, weather conditions, and assistance level used.

What is the maximum speed of ENGWE e-bikes? ▼

Most ENGWE e-bikes have a maximum assisted speed of 20 mph (32 km/h) in the US, complying with Class 2 e-bike regulations. Some models offer:

  • Pedal Assist Mode: Up to 20 mph
  • Throttle Mode: Up to 20 mph
  • Manual Pedaling: No speed limit (rider dependent)

Speed may vary by region due to local regulations.

Are ENGWE e-bikes suitable for tall/short riders? ▼

ENGWE e-bikes are designed to accommodate a wide range of rider heights:

  • Recommended Height Range: 5'1" - 6'5" (155cm - 195cm)
  • Adjustable Components: Seat height, handlebar angle
  • Step-Through Designs: Available on select models for easier mounting
  • Folding Models: P20 offers compact design with adjustability

For specific height recommendations by model, please contact our customer service team.

What's the difference between fat tire and regular tire e-bikes? ▼

Fat tire e-bikes offer several advantages:

Fat Tire Benefits:

  • Better traction on sand, snow, and loose surfaces
  • Improved shock absorption and comfort
  • Enhanced stability and control
  • Suitable for all-terrain riding

Regular Tire Benefits:

  • Lower rolling resistance (better efficiency)
  • Lighter weight
  • Better for paved roads and commuting
  • Easier to find replacement parts

🔧 Technical Support

How do I maintain my ENGWE e-bike? ▼

Regular maintenance ensures optimal performance and longevity:

Monthly Maintenance:

  • Check tire pressure and tread wear
  • Inspect brake pads and adjust if needed
  • Clean and lubricate chain
  • Check all bolts and connections
  • Clean battery contacts

Battery Care:

  • Charge regularly, don't let battery fully drain
  • Store in cool, dry place (32°F to 77°F)
  • Remove battery if storing bike for extended periods
Maintenance Battery Care Tips
What should I do if my e-bike won't turn on? ▼

Try these troubleshooting steps:

  1. Check Battery: Ensure battery is charged and properly connected
  2. Power Button: Hold power button for 3-5 seconds
  3. Display Connection: Check display unit connections
  4. Fuse Check: Inspect fuse in battery compartment
  5. Key Switch: Ensure key is turned to ON position (if equipped)

If these steps don't resolve the issue, contact our technical support team for further assistance.
